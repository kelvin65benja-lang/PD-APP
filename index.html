← Help & FAQ
How do I call a service provider?

Open Services, find the business, and tap the green Call button. If your device can't open the dialer, the number is copied automatically so you can paste it.

How do I report a community problem?

Go to Report, choose the issue type, add the location and description, then tap Send via SMS. This works even with a weak connection.

How do I become a verified Admin?

Go to Settings → Administrator Access and enter your passcode. Ask the app owner for your passcode.

Is my phone number shared publicly?

No. Your number stays on this device and is only used to open your own dialer, WhatsApp, or SMS apps.

Can I use the app without internet?

Yes. Previously loaded listings, weather and your favorites are all saved on your device for offline use.
